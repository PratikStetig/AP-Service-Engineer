General Practice for the Recommended coating system

After considering the conditions like degree of rusting, degree of blistering and other coating failures the following coating system proposal is recommended:

9.1 General Surface Preparation*

  • Ensure Relative humidity close to job structure less than 80%.
  • Temporary weld supports, weld spatter etc., should be removed prior to surface preparation. These are weak areas for corrosion to start.
  • To remove all oil, Grease, fine powder due to chalking by Solvent Cleaning as per SSPC SP-1. It is recommended to use Thinner - T131 or thinner 90-53.
  • Impact Tools like Hammer to be used to remove heavy scaling prior to proceed with further surface preparations.
  • All loose paint, rusted part, Blister & delimitation are removed up to surface by using power tool.
  • Hand tool cleaning permissible for limited areas due to poor access or safety factor.
  • Low pressure water jetting shall be performed to remove invisible salt contaminants from the surface.
  • Existing coating DFT to be recorded in all areas for record prior to primer application.
  • Ensure the painting should be carried out in less than 3 Hrs. from the surface preparation

This is the general guidelines to prepare the surface before painting for specific and detailed surface preparation guidelines refer technical data sheet of the product specified.
